50 HIGHEST-RATED DAVE MELTZER MATCHES — COVER IMAGE COLLECTION
Prepared 15 October 2026.

50 JPG images, numbered to match sources.csv. Images retain their original proportions and resolution.
The collection uses a mixture of match photographs, promotional match graphics and event posters.
The Flair/Steamboat image is a rivalry illustration used by the source article; it is not verified as a photograph of the untelevised Landover bout.

Selection: rating descending, then match date ascending (oldest first); same-day ties alphabetically by match name. The cutoff is 5.5 stars. There are more matches tied at this rating than can fit in a 50-image collection.
Match dates are dates held/taped, which can differ from broadcast dates (e.g. Alexander/Ospreay taped 14 January and aired 18 January 2024).
Ratings and match list source:
https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_professional_wrestling_matches_rated_5_or_more_stars_by_Dave_Meltzer
Ratings are reported historical ratings, which can differ between compilations or later revisions. This is not an official ranked list published by Dave Meltzer.

sources.csv records the exact source page and image URL for every image. Rights remain with the respective owners; sourcing the files does not grant a reuse licence.
